## Art Direction & Style Guide

All staff avatars follow the **Modern Editorial Line Vector** standard:
- **Medium**: Modern editorial line-art vector illustration (*The New Yorker* / *Monocle* / *Architectural Digest* style).
- **Technique**: Crisp clean black outlines with subtle screen-printed halftone dot shading.
- **Palette**: Warm ivory background (`#F8F5EE`), French navy (`#1B2A4A`), warm charcoal, muted terracotta, and brass gold accents.
- **Framing**: Centered in a thin circular border ring on a solid off-white background (optimized for Discord's circular crop).
- **Vibe**: *"Very professional, effortlessly stylish, and slightly too attractive for the job."*
